# Cozymeal teardown

Researched 2026-10-16. Basis labels: [documented] [reported] [inferred].

Scope: cooking classes, food tours, tastings. Pages read as text; one tour page also viewed at 375px in the browser pane (scroll only). Checkout was not entered, so anything past the detail page is "not verified (needs a live checkout)".

## 1. Snapshot

- Marketplace for food and drink experiences: classes, private chefs, food tours, team building, online classes, tastings, gift cards. [documented] ([homepage](https://www.cozymeal.com/))
- Self-reported: 50,000+ experiences in 400+ cities, since 2014. US metros plus Canada, France, Italy, Portugal, Spain, UAE sites. [documented] ([homepage](https://www.cozymeal.com/)). Unaudited marketing figures.
- Travelers vs locals: no audience data. Listings read as local and occasion driven (date night, team building, "At your place" classes). [inferred] ([SF classes](https://www.cozymeal.com/san-francisco/cooking-classes))

## 2. Fees and business model

| Item | Finding | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Money flow | Guest pays Cozymeal; Cozymeal's third-party payment processor holds funds in escrow until 24 hours after the event's scheduled end; Cozymeal remits the Contributions "following the Event" less its Host Service Fee and any Rental Fees ([Terms](https://www.cozymeal.com/legal/terms-of-use) §4(b), §4(j)). Listing pages brand this the "Cozymeal Escrow System" ([Mission tour](https://www.cozymeal.com/food-tour/21841/exploring-the-mission-district)) | [documented] |
| Merchant of record | Terms call Cozymeal a venue connecting hosts and guests, not a party to their arrangement (§1). MoR status not stated | not verified |
| Processors | Stripe, PayPal, Apple Pay ([Terms](https://www.cozymeal.com/legal/terms-of-use) §4(q)) | [documented] |
| Guest fees | The Terms describe no guest fee beyond the Contribution (§4(a)); the only "Service Fee" in the Terms is on venue rentals (§4(f)). Hosts set their own pricing and include service in their rates; tipping is not expected and is optional after the event; chefs may add travel or private-upgrade fees ([FAQ](https://www.cozymeal.com/faq)). Most of the 17 listing pages read show no fee line, but 4 show "Service Fee $ 4.99" in the price-summary block (totals read $0 until a date and guest count are chosen): [Detroit team building](https://www.cozymeal.com/team-building/22955/traditional-mexican-cooking), [Tampa class](https://www.cozymeal.com/cooking-class/28746/sushi-making--date-night), [Tampa date-night class](https://www.cozymeal.com/cooking-class/45264/date-night-coastal-italian-feast), [Des Moines private chef](https://www.cozymeal.com/private-chef/22161/gourmet-steakhouse-fare). No Cozymeal page or outside source explains it. Whether it is shown and charged at checkout: not verified (needs a live checkout) | [documented]; checkout not verified |
| Host commission | "Host Service Fee"; the rate is not in the Terms and is "listed in your profile page available via your Member Account" ([Terms](https://www.cozymeal.com/legal/terms-of-use) §4(b), §4(e)), so it is not public. The become-a-chef page gives no rate and says "Free to join" ([become a chef](https://www.cozymeal.com/become-a-chef)); whether listing is free is not stated | [documented] |

No Cozymeal page corroborates it (not verified) | [reported] |
| Venues | Cozymeal-arranged rental costs plus "Cozymeal's Service Fee" are deducted from guest Contributions; the host reimburses any Rental Fees above the Contributions ([Terms](https://www.cozymeal.com/legal/terms-of-use) §4(f)) | [documented] |
| Price display | Per person, "all-inclusive", with tax wording varying by listing type. Tours: "all-inclusive: 5 food stops and taxes" ([Mission tour](https://www.cozymeal.com/food-tour/21841/exploring-the-mission-district)) and "4 food stops and taxes" ([Chinatown tour](https://www.cozymeal.com/food-tour/18095/explore-chinatown-and-little-italy)). Classes and team building: "all-inclusive: 3-hour class, N-course meal" with no tax wording ([NY pizza](https://www.cozymeal.com/cooking-class/5238/new-york-style-pizza), [Italian class](https://www.cozymeal.com/cooking-class/10034/classic-italian-with-modern-influences)). Some private chef pages show "All inclusive" plus itemized "Sales Tax (8.88%)" and "Retail Delivery Fee" lines (both $0 until booking details are entered) and per-person rates that fall with group size, e.g. $119 for 2 guests and $99 for 6 to 15 ([NYC private chef](https://www.cozymeal.com/private-chef/37358/a-festival-of-french-flavors)); the [FAQ](https://www.cozymeal.com/faq) says rates do not decrease for larger groups, while the [private chef cost page](https://cozymeal.com/faq/personal-chef-costs) (updated 2025-10-04) says per-guest price falls as the party grows. Terms say listed prices include sales tax (§4(o)). Kits: "$85/Person + Kit" is one of 12 kit prices shown, $55 to $115 ([kit page](https://www.cozymeal.com/lp/kit-classes)); the FAQ says a virtual kit total is the class price (kit included) plus tax by recipient address plus shipping. What "+ Kit" means and whether tax and shipping show before checkout: not verified (needs a live checkout) | [documented] |
| Payout timing | Remitted "following the Event", after the escrow window (24 hours after scheduled end); no schedule beyond that ([Terms](https://www.cozymeal.com/legal/terms-of-use) §4(b), §4(j)) | [documented] |

## 5. Takeaways

- Most listings show no guest fee; per-person "all-inclusive" pricing carries the economics and the host fee sits behind the scenes. [inferred] Exception: a flat "Service Fee $ 4.99" line sits in the price summary on 4 of 17 listings read, charge at checkout not verified. [documented]
- One 48-hour line is the dominant trust signal (12 of 17 listings read), repeated across cards, detail pages, and email. Exceptions are split: kits and last-minute rules sit on a separate legal page, while tiered private chef policies and team-building venue fees sit on the listing itself. [documented]
- Dated slots (BOOK NOW) and request flows (custom dates, private events) coexist, and company copy still describes request-to-book. [documented]
- Scarcity cues are soft social proof ("Booked 460+ times", "N People Attending", "SOLD OUT"), no timer. [documented]
- Reviews cluster on host-side cancellations and thin remedies; the host-cancel refund rule is unpublished. [reported]
