# CourseHorse and ClassBento teardown

Researched 2026-10-16. Basis labels: [documented] [reported] [inferred].

Live pages were read at a 375px viewport. Nothing was booked and no checkout was entered.

## 1. Snapshot

- **CourseHorse:** US marketplace for classes, workshops, tours and tastings from independent schools and instructors. NYC, LA, Chicago, a dozen-plus other cities, and online. 13,000+ classes in the past year. Browsed by city, so mostly locals. [documented: [home](https://coursehorse.com/), [teach page](https://coursehorse.com/list-your-classes)]
- **ClassBento:** separate UK (17,000+ experiences), Australia (16,000+) and US (9,000+) sites. Art, craft, food and drink workshops, team events, heavy gift card push. Locals and gift buyers; no traveler targeting found. [documented: [UK](https://classbento.co.uk/), [AU](https://classbento.com.au/), [US](https://classbento.com/); traveler point inferred]

## 5. Takeaways

- Neither platform sets a default policy. ClassBento forces a structured label; CourseHorse shows free text that varies widely.
- Credit beats cash: CourseHorse listings often give credit only, and most US ClassBento listings sampled allow reschedule but not cancel (driven by online kits; in-person US listings split 4 to 3 for free cancel).
- Platform fees sit on the supply side, and guests see listed prices at booking. Guest-borne charges appear on cancellation: a 5% processing fee on CourseHorse full refunds and provider fees such as $10 same day or 15% administrative.
- The policy sits below the fold or at checkout, not in the sticky bar.

| | CourseHorse | ClassBento |
|---|---|---|
| Guest fee | None claimed at booking; 5% off full refunds on all 9 listings opened | None found |
| Provider fee | Per enrollment, undisclosed | 20%; 3.9% plus fixed on widget |
| Payout | 1st and 15th | 1 business day after class |
| Policy | Provider free text | Provider tier label |
